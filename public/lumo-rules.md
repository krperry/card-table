# Lumo Rules

## Objective

Be the first player to get rid of all the cards in your hand.

## Setup

- Shuffle all 116 cards.
- Deal 7 cards to each player.
- Place the remaining cards face down as the Draw Pile.
- Turn over the top card to begin the Discard Pile.
- If the first card is an Action or Wild card, resolve it using the
  standard rules below.

## Taking a Turn

- Play a card that matches the current color, or
- Play a card that matches the current number or symbol, or
- Play a Wild card.

If you cannot play: - Draw one card. - If it can be played, you may play
it immediately. - Otherwise your turn ends.

## Number Cards

Number cards (0 through 9) have no special effect.

## Action Cards

### Skip

The next player loses their turn.

### Reverse

Reverse the direction of play. In a two-player game, Reverse acts the
same as Skip.

### Draw +2

The next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn. A Draw +2 may only
be played on a matching color or another Draw +2.

### Give +1

Choose one card from your hand. Pass it in the current direction of play
to the next player. The color of the Give +1 card remains the active
color.

**Special Rule**

If playing Give +1 leaves you with only one card in your hand, you may
not give away your final remaining card.

Instead: - Play the Give +1. - Say "Lumo!" if required. - Keep your last
card. - Play continues.

A Give +1 card can never be used to give away your final card.

## Wild

Play at any time and choose the new color.

## Wild Draw +4

Play only if you have no card matching the current color. Choose the new
color. The next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn.

A player may challenge a Wild Draw +4.

- If the challenge succeeds, the player who played it draws 4 cards.
- If the challenge fails, the challenger draws 6 cards.

## Calling "Lumo"

When you have one card remaining you must say: **Lumo!**

## Winning

You win immediately when you play your last card.

**Exception**

A Give +1 card cannot be used to give away your final remaining card.

## Optional Scoring

  ----------------------
  Card            Points
  ------------ ---------
  Number Cards      Face
                   Value

  Skip                20

  Reverse             20

  Draw +2             20

  Give +1             20

  Wild                50

  Wild Draw +4        50
  ----------------------

Game hosts may choose the victory condition before the game begins. A
game may end after a selected number of rounds, when a selected point
total is reached, or using any other scoring option supported by the
game.

**Suggested target scores:** the winning-score setting has a big effect on
how long a match runs, especially with fewer players. These are
recommendations, not requirements - a table can use any allowed score, and
players wanting a shorter game should just pick a lower target:

- **2 players:** 100-200 points
- **3-4 players:** 200-400 points
- **5-6 players:** around 500 points

Higher target scores mean longer games, particularly at the low end of the
player-count range.
